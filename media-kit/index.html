Community gathering under the LOVE FORWARD neon — Creative Haven / event energy.

Mission

Love Forward Foundation supports children, adult children, caregivers, and families impacted by parental incarceration — centering dignity, belonging, and lived experience.

Families Serve Sentences Too™

When a parent is incarcerated, families serve the sentence too. Love Forward builds pathways so children, adult children, caregivers, and families impacted by parental incarceration are seen, supported, and connected to resources, relationships, and opportunities.

  • Visibility — families impacted by parental incarceration deserve to be seen
  • Dignity — healing-centered, trauma-informed care without stigma
  • Connection — community rituals, workshops, and support spaces
  • Community Care — building understanding and support across caregivers, families, and partners
  • Lived Experience Leadership — led by those who know the journey

Programs & impact

What Love Forward Foundation is building for families affected by parental incarceration — plus featured initiative and community roots.

What We're Building

In development

Family Stability Portal

An interactive digital family-stability navigation system designed for families navigating parental incarceration. Instead of requiring families to know what organization or service they need, the Portal begins with one simple question: “What is happening with your family right now?” From there, families are guided toward practical information, tools, resources, and support based on what they are actually experiencing.

Coming

The Shared Sentence Experience

An experiential and educational framework that brings the impact of parental incarceration to life through the experiences of children, adult children, caregivers, and families serving the sentence on the outside.

Primary funded work · 2026

Project SEEN

Seeing the Needs of Children and Caregivers Impacted by Parental Incarceration

Project SEEN is our primary funded work for 2026. It focuses on identifying and responding to the needs of children and caregivers impacted by parental incarceration, especially needs that can easily go unseen when families do not disclose what they are experiencing. The work includes practical family tools and resources, access support, community education, partnerships, and connections to resources. For 2026, Project SEEN is funded through support from the Community Foundation and the Kiwanis Club.

Built in Community

For more than a decade, Love Forward Foundation has created spaces where families can connect, create, learn, and feel seen. Our community-based work helped shape what we are building today.

Creative Haven · history

Creative Haven created welcoming spaces for families to connect through creativity, conversation, and shared experiences. The lessons learned through this work helped shape Love Forward Foundation's continued focus on family stability, access, connection, and support.
