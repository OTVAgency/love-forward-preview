What we do

Three pillars. One movement.

Advocacy & Awareness

Make justice-impacted families visible — especially in rural Virginia.

Connection & Support

Workshops, creative coping, and pathways so families feel seen and less alone.

Ecosystem Building

Reentry and community care that includes families on the outside.

Featured initiative

Project SEEN

Seeing the Needs of Children and Caregivers Impacted by Parental Incarceration. Pathways to identify, support, and connect families to resources and relationships. Supported by the Waynesboro Kiwanis Club.
