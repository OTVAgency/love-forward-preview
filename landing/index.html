What we do

Three pillars. One movement.

From the foundation’s brochure and community work: advocacy that makes families visible, connection that ends isolation, and ecosystems that include the people serving the sentence on the outside.

Advocacy & Awareness

Raise statewide awareness of the shared sentence and protect the stories of justice-impacted families — especially in rural communities.

Connection & Support

Weekly workshops, creative coping rituals, and pathways that help caregivers, youth, and adult children feel seen, supported, and less alone.

Ecosystem Building

Strengthen reentry and community care by including families — building relationships, resources, and sanctuaries that did not exist before.

Featured initiative

Project SEEN

Seeing the Needs of Children and Caregivers Impacted by Parental Incarceration.

When a parent is incarcerated, families serve the sentence too. Through Project SEEN, Love Forward is building stronger pathways to identify, support, and connect these families to resources, relationships, and opportunities.

Supported by the Waynesboro Kiwanis Club. Because families serving a shared sentence deserve to be seen.

About

Founded in lived experience

Love Forward Foundation is a justice-rooted nonprofit serving the Shenandoah Valley and Virginia statewide. The work centers healing-centered curriculum, creative coping, community rituals, and digital storytelling so emotional truth is honored — not hidden.

Keisha Nicholson

Founder & Executive Director

Keisha’s leadership is rooted in the lived experience of parental incarceration. Early work began with pen pals and care packages during visitation, grew into creative workshops and a brick-and-mortar Creative Haven in downtown Staunton, and continues as a family-focused, caregiver-centered movement — including outreach in schools, facilities, and community events. Her aim, as she has put it: foster resilience through self-reliance.

Creative Haven

A creative-coping space for youth and caregivers — art, hair, writing, candles, jewelry, music, and more — with workshops that often end in a youth market where young people sell what they made.

Rituals & Ritual Guide

Mind, body, and spiritual practices already on the live site: Pick a Truth, Write + Release, Ground with a Heart, Tie a Thread — plus a Ritual Playlist for soft resets.

Community events
